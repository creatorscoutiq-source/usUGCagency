# Outreach Email Templates — Tech UGC & Canvas UGC
Oct 23, 2026 · @shubh

## The offer behind every email
Every template opens on a pain the buyer already feels, then answers it with the same offer: one creator, up to 60 videos a month, $2,000/month, about $33 a video. The CTA is always "want to see the creators' videos and pick who you'd work with?"

| What we bring | How it shows up in the email |
|---|---|
| Up to 60 videos/creator/month at $2,000/month | The price line. It's the answer to "agencies charge thousands" |
| Creators with mostly Tier 1 country audiences | Kills the "cheap offshore content" objection early |
| Live deals with Higgsfield and VEED.io | One line of proof: "we're currently producing for Higgsfield and VEED.io" |
| Shocked-hook reels, explanation reels, voiceover reels, Canvas UGC | Name 2–3 formats matched to their product |
| Brand picks creators from their real videos | The CTA. Low-commitment, and it shifts the talk from "should we" to "which ones" |

## Which template to use

| Prospect looks like | Template |
|---|---|
| Already uses a US UGC agency or runs agency-style Canvas UGC ads | 1: Overpaying |
| Posts campaigns on Sideshift or other creator marketplaces | 2: Marketplace quality |
| Works through an agency or talent manager (any region) | 3: Markup |
| Publicly recruiting and paying creators themselves (P1) | 4: In-house ops |
| Small founder-led app, any signal | 5: Founder short |

Placeholders: {{first_name}}, {{company}}, {{product}}, {{formats}} (2–3 of our reel types that fit them), {{audience}}.

## Template 1: Overpaying a US agency

**Subject options:** still paying thousands for Canvas UGC? · {{company}}'s UGC bill · 60 videos for $2,000

```
Hi {{first_name}},

Is a US agency charging you thousands of dollars a month for your Canvas UGC?

Most of that is agency overhead, not creative. We do it differently: one of our creators makes up to 60 videos a month for {{product}} for $2,000, all aimed at Tier 1 audiences.

We're currently producing for Higgsfield and VEED.io, mostly {{formats}}.

Want me to send a few of our creators' videos so you can pick who you'd like to work with?

{{sender_name}}
```

> Works best when you've seen polished, agency-style UGC in their ads. Don't send it to someone with no creator content at all.

## Template 2: Marketplace creators aren't good enough

**Subject options:** creator quality on Sideshift · tired of sorting through creators? · creators who can actually hook

```
Hi {{first_name}},

Even with Sideshift, are you still struggling to find creators who can actually hold attention in the first two seconds?

Marketplaces give you volume of applicants, not quality. You still end up screening, briefing, and re-shooting.

Our creators specialise in tech content: {{formats}}. Each one can deliver up to 60 videos a month for $2,000, with Tier 1 audiences. We're doing this right now for Higgsfield and VEED.io.

If you're hiring creators at the moment, I can send our creators' videos and you pick the ones you'd want on {{product}}.

{{sender_name}}
```

> Only name Sideshift (or any marketplace) when you've actually seen them post a campaign there. Otherwise say "creator marketplaces".

## Template 3: The markup on creator pay

**Subject options:** what your creator actually gets paid · fun fact about your UGC agency · the UGC markup

```
Hi {{first_name}},

Fun fact: in a lot of UGC agency setups, the brand pays roughly double what the creator actually takes home.

So you're paying for the creator twice, and the person making your videos still isn't the one being paid properly.

We keep it simple. One creator, up to 60 videos a month for {{product}}, $2,000 flat. Mostly Tier 1 audiences, and we're running this for Higgsfield and VEED.io right now.

Should I send over a few creators' videos so you can see who'd fit?

{{sender_name}}
```

> Keep the markup line general ("a lot of agency setups"). Never tell a prospect their agency is overcharging them, since we can't see their contract.

## Template 4: You've become your own UGC agency

For P1 companies recruiting creators themselves. The pain: running the program has turned their marketer into a full-time creator manager.

**Subject options:** you're running a UGC agency now · {{N}} creators to manage? · one creator, 60 videos

```
Hi {{first_name}},

Hiring creators yourself means {{company}} is basically running its own UGC agency: sourcing, briefing, chasing drafts, and replacing whoever goes quiet after week two.

What if one creator covered 60 videos a month for $2,000? Tier 1 audiences, {{formats}}, and it's exactly what we're doing for Higgsfield and VEED.io right now.

Since you're already hiring, want me to send our creators' videos so you can shortlist the ones you like?

{{sender_name}}
```

> If their post needs creators posting on their own US accounts, check the roster covers it first. If not, pitch videos for their brand accounts and ads.

## Pain-point hooks and subject lines

### Opening hooks
- "Is your US agency charging you thousands for Canvas UGC that your team could brief in an afternoon?"
- "Even Sideshift can't seem to find creators who nail the hook. Sound familiar?"
- "Fun fact: you may be paying your agency double what your creator takes home."
- "How many of the creators you hired last month actually delivered every video?"
- "What would you do with 60 new videos a month instead of 6?"
- "Your ads are running the same three creators. When did you last test a new face?"
- "Paying {{their_rate}} per UGC video? We do it for about $33." *(Only use when their rate is public, e.g. in a hiring post.)*

### Subject lines
- still paying thousands for UGC?
- 60 videos / $2,000
- your agency's markup
- better creators than Sideshift
- {{company}} x Higgsfield-style UGC
- creators for {{product}}, pick yours

## Follow-ups

Two follow-ups and a close-out, in the same thread.

### Follow-up 1 (day 3): send the creators

```
Hi {{first_name}},

Rather than wait, here are 3 creators I'd put on {{product}}:

{{creator_1}}: {{link}} (shocked-hook reels)
{{creator_2}}: {{link}} (explanation reels)
{{creator_3}}: {{link}} (voiceover reels)

Any of them can do up to 60 videos a month for $2,000. Just tell me which one you like and I'll set up a first batch.

{{sender_name}}
```

### Follow-up 2 (day 7): re-state the pain, smaller ask

```
Hi {{first_name}},

One more angle: what's {{company}} paying per video right now, all-in?

If it's more than about $33, it's worth a 10-minute look at the creators above. If someone else handles creator content, I'd appreciate a pointer.

{{sender_name}}
```

### Close-out (day 14)

```
Hi {{first_name}},

I'll leave it here. If you ever want 60 videos a month without the agency markup, reply to this and I'll send fresh creator picks for {{product}}.

{{sender_name}}
```

## Guardrails before sending

- [ ] Confirm Higgsfield and VEED.io are fine with being named in cold email. If not, say "two AI video tools" instead.
- [ ] The pain you lead with matches what you saw: an agency for template 1, a marketplace for template 2, creator job posts for template 4.
- [ ] The markup and "thousands" lines stay general, never a claim about this prospect's specific contract.
- [ ] The prospect actually pays for content (P1), or you're using template 5 as an exploratory send. No affiliate-only programs.
- [ ] Geography fits: if they need US creators posting on their own accounts, the roster covers it, or you pitch brand-owned/ad content instead.
- [ ] You have 3 creator videos ready to send before the email goes out, so follow-up 1 is instant.
- [ ] Under ~100 words, one question as the CTA.
